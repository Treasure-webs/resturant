EMBER KITCHEN

Open index.html in a browser. No installation, build process or internet connection is needed.

Files:
  index.html    Page content and labelled order controls
  styles.css    Responsive design; adjust the colour variables at the top
  script.js     Food prices, validation, order calculation and menu selection
  images/       Downloaded food photography

Prices: Chicken NGN 5,000; Rice Bowl NGN 3,500; Burger NGN 4,500; Pasta NGN 4,000.
Delivery: Pickup NGN 0; nearby NGN 500; far NGN 1,500. Charged once per order.

This is a fictional restaurant. Replace the sample address, telephone and hours before publishing.
The calculator displays a price estimate and does not send an order or collect payment.
